Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Showcase Detailing LLC, doing business as Showcase Boat Detailing ("we," "us"), is a mobile boat detailing service in Volusia & Flagler Counties, Florida. This policy explains what information we collect when you request a quote or book a service, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you contact us — through the quote form on this website, a Facebook or Instagram lead form, or by phone or text — we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your city and where your boat is located (dock, lift, marina, dry storage, or driveway)
  • Details about your boat and the service you are asking about, such as boat type and length, gelcoat condition, and photos you send us
  • Anything else you include in your message to us

How we use it

  • To respond to your quote request and answer your questions
  • To schedule and provide the service you book, including booking confirmations and appointment reminders
  • To follow up about your service, including maintenance reminders when your boat is due and a request for a review after a job
  • To keep records of the work we have done on your boat

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the service providers we use to run the business — such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram) for lead forms, Square for booking and payments, and Google for email — and only as needed for them to provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Texts and calls

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your quote, booking, or service. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying STOP to a text or telling us directly, and we will stop.

How long we keep it

We keep customer contact and service records for as long as needed to provide service, follow up on maintenance, and meet legal and accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us at booking@showcaseboatdetailing.com or (386) 315-3633 and we will take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
