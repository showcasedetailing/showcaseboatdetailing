Flagship

Buff & Shine

Our flagship: oxidation removal plus a full topside cleanup, sold together unless you ask to split it. Buffing makes a mess — a half-cleaned boat isn't a finished boat. Staged machine correction takes dull, chalky gelcoat back to a deep, wet gloss, then the whole topside gets cleaned and finished.

  • Gelcoat condition assessment
  • Staged cut & polish — oxidation and haze removal
  • Full topside cleanup and finishing
  • Protective sealant to lock in the gloss

From $45/ft — boats under 25 ft. Two-day jobs cost more. Heavy oxidation extra.
